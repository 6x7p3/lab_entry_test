# Provisional annotation evidence

## Scope and method

- Input: `/workspace/cortex_clustered.h5ad`, 3,005 cells × 19,972 features, `obs['leiden']`; ranked cluster-versus-rest table `/workspace/cortex_markers.csv` (30 genes per cluster); symbols from `var['genes']`. A mouse cortex/hippocampus origin is plausible from the supplied context and gene nomenclature, but **neither species nor tissue has been formally authenticated**. All assignments are provisional. No known sample-ID column was available, so no across-sample reproducibility assessment was possible.
- Predictions were derived independently from marker statistics and the literature-informed panels below. The existing `label` and `label2` columns were not consulted or evaluated, and were preserved. These are not labels transferred from a reference atlas; there was no automated reference mapping.
- `X` metadata records `normalize_total_log1p` with target 10,000; counts are in `layers['counts']`. Detection means `X > 0` for a gene. For every cluster and panel gene, `/workspace/cortex_reference_panel_metrics.csv` reports inside and outside detection fractions, mean log1p expression, and an approximate log2 fold change: log2[(expm1(mean log1p in)+1e-9)/(expm1(mean log1p out)+1e-9)]. These are group-versus-all-other-cells comparisons, not a sample-level effect or a direct candidate-versus-candidate statistical test. Near-zero detection can lead to extreme fold changes. Rank-table log2FCs match this convention to rounding; ranks are Wilcoxon scores, not causal evidence.
- Panels covered neuronal (Snap25, Slc17a7, Gad1/2, Slc32a1), interneuron subtype (Sst, Vip, Pvalb, Reln, Lhx6), excitatory subtype (Neurod6, Bcl11b, Crym, Tbr1, Cux2, Prox1), astrocyte (Slc1a2/3, Aqp4, Gja1, Gpr37l1), oligodendrocyte/OPC (Mbp, Mog, Mobp, Plp1, Mag, Pdgfra, Cspg4), endothelial (Cldn5, Ptprb, Flt1, Pecam1), mural (Acta2, Tagln, Myh11, Rgs5, Pdgfrb), and immune (P2ry12, Cx3cr1, Tmem119, Mrc1, Pf4, Lyve1) markers, including markers absent from a cluster's 30 top ranks. All panel symbols were found. `Mar-01` and `Mar-02` are duplicated symbols, but neither is used in these panels; each examined panel symbol was matched to its first occurrence.
- In the table below, `in/out` is the fraction detected in the cluster versus the remaining cells, and `LFC` is the approximate log2 fold change against the rest. Fractions and LFCs are rounded. Low or shared expression of a single marker does not establish absence, identity or subtype.

## Measured mixed-lineage comparisons

A panel-positive cell has detected expression of at least **two of three** genes: endothelial (Cldn5, Ptprb, Flt1), oligodendrocyte (Mog, Mobp, Mag), astrocyte (Slc1a3, Gja1, Aqp4), or inhibitory (Gad1, Gad2, Slc32a1). These ad hoc detection rules are descriptive, not validated doublet classifiers; sporadic background can satisfy them.

- **13:** 58/80 endothelial-positive, 73/80 oligodendrocyte-positive, and 58/80 positive for both. Comparator endothelial cluster 9 has 21/244 both, and oligodendrocyte cluster 17 has 7/428 both. Co-detection is therefore prominent within individual cluster 13 cells, not merely a group-average artifact.
- **15:** 48/48 astrocyte-positive and 48/48 oligodendrocyte-positive, hence 48/48 both. Comparator astrocyte cluster 4 has 13/102 both, cluster 6 has 10/165, and oligodendrocyte cluster 17 has 79/428. Background/co-detection exists in comparators, but is far less universal.
- **1:** 52/76 inhibitory-positive and 31/76 astrocyte-positive; 8/76 are both. Thus 44 are inhibitory-only, 23 astrocyte-only, 8 both and 1 neither by these rules. This points to cluster heterogeneity, with possible mixed cells, rather than treating the Sst signature as representative of all cells. Clusters 0 and 6 have 8/129 and 6/165 inhibitory-plus-astrocyte-positive, respectively.
- Biological lineage coexpression, technical RNA contamination, multiplets or clustering artifacts can produce conflict; no cause is proven by these data. Follow-up: sample identity and batch-aware reanalysis, cell-level embedding and counts, ambient-RNA and doublet diagnostics, and independent markers or imaging. No cells were removed on this basis.

## Mapping and validation

`annotations.csv` contains one row for each of 18 observed cluster IDs. IDs were converted to strings for mapping, all 3,005 cells received exactly one `predicted_cell_type`, and the saved h5ad was reloaded to verify every cluster's expected count and label. The 3 unresolved clusters contain 204/3,005 cells (6.8%). All pre-existing `obs` columns, including supplied labels, were checked for exact equality after saving; original labels were not compared to predictions. Cluster and UMAP are inherited from the input; no new clustering or embedding was performed. No sample-wise consistency or biological ground-truth validation can be claimed.
